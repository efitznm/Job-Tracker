### Micro-Internship: Data Entry and File Organization VII at Parker Dewey

## Job Description
Project Description

The Data Entry Micro-Intern will review case records in LegalServer (NMILC's web-based case management database) and make changes as directed to keep case files accurate and up-to-date.

The successful Micro-Intern will organize DropBox files for 150 LegalServer Case profiles with the goal of maintaining accurate and complete client records. Upon completion of this project, the intern will be able to add the following skills to their resume:

- LegalServer case management database
- Ability to cross-reference diverse client data points to maintain seamless integration with electronic filing systems.
- Identify process improvements for auditing large quantities of client data and files

Company Description

Our mission is to advance justice and equity by empowering low-income immigrant communities through collaborative legal services, advocacy, and education.
