# Business & Administration Intern

Job Description
Position Summary 

The Business & Administration Intern provides administrative, operational, financial, and data management support to Boys & Girls Clubs of Central New Mexico. This position offers hands-on experience in nonprofit management by exposing students to multiple areas of organizational operations, including administration, human resources, finance, membership services, data management, and program evaluation. 

The Intern will assist staff with day-to-day operations, data tracking, record management, reporting, and special projects while gaining valuable experience in nonprofit administration and organizational leadership. This position is ideal for students interested in business administration, accounting, human resources, nonprofit management, public administration, data analytics, or related fields. 

Nonprofit Operations & Administration 

Rotate through Development, Programs, Operations, Finance, Human Resources, and Administration departments to gain exposure to nonprofit operations. 
Assist with administrative projects, document preparation, filing, scheduling, and general office support. 
Support special projects and organization-wide initiatives as assigned. 
Human Resources Support 

Assist with recruitment activities, including job postings, applicant tracking, and interview scheduling. 
Support onboarding processes, employee paperwork, and personnel file organization. 
Maintain confidentiality of employee and organizational information. 
Finance & Accounting Support 

Assist with accounts payable and receivable processes, expense tracking, and financial record organization under staff supervision. 
Support budget projects, reconciliations, and documentation of financial transactions. 
Maintain accurate financial and administrative records. 
Data, Membership & Impact Support 

Assist with membership records, registrations, attendance tracking, and database maintenance. 
Support data entry, reporting, and data quality initiatives using organizational systems. 
Assist with program outcome tracking, dashboards, grant reporting, and impact measurement activities. 
Compile data and reports to support organizational decision-making and funding requirements. 
Professional Development 

Participate in department meetings, staff trainings, and professional development opportunities. 
Gain hands-on experience in nonprofit administration, organizational management, and business operations. 
Perform other duties and special projects as assigned. 

Why Join BGCCNM? 

At Boys & Girls Clubs of Central New Mexico, our mission is to enable all young people, especially those who need us most, to reach their full potential as productive, caring, and responsible citizens. Every role at BGCCNM contributes to creating safe, supportive environments where youth can succeed academically, build healthy lifestyles, develop leadership skills, and prepare for successful futures. Through innovative programs, caring adult mentors, and strong community partnerships, we help young people discover their strengths and achieve their goals. 

We are committed to investing in our employees through meaningful work, professional development, hands-on learning opportunities, and pathways for growth. Whether you are beginning your career, gaining experience through an internship, or bringing established expertise to our team, BGCCNM offers the opportunity to make a lasting impact in the lives of youth while helping create Great Futures throughout Central New Mexico.

Interest in nonprofit work, youth development, education, public administration, business administration, accounting, human resources, data analytics, or a related field. Experience volunteering, working, or participating in youth, school, or community-based programs preferred. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work both independently and collaboratively. Basic proficiency with Microsoft Office 365, including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint. Ability to successfully complete required background screenings. Reliable method of transportation. Familiarity with Albuquerque communities, schools, or nonprofit organizations. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Strong interpersonal and professional communication skills. Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail. Ability to manage multiple assignments and meet deadlines. Ability to learn new software systems and administrative processes. Adaptability, initiative, and willingness to take on new responsibilities.
