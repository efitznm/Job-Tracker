Business Development Intern
Location: Nmexus Center - Albuquerque, NM

Position Type: Part-Time Internship (Flexible Schedule)

Reports To: Globalization Specialist and Executive Leadership

Position Summary
Nmexus Center is seeking a motivated Business Development Intern to assist with identifying business opportunities, building relationships with local organizations, and supporting initiatives that help businesses grow and succeed. This internship provides hands-on experience working with startups, entrepreneurs, established businesses, and community partners while learning how business development drives economic growth.

This is an excellent opportunity for students interested in entrepreneurship, business growth, networking, and community development.
Interns may also have opportunities to collaborate with other Nmexus Center departments on selected
projects. This cross-functional exposure is designed to help students understand how different business
functions work together and explore other career areas that may interest them.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities
• Assist with business development initiatives that support the growth of Nmexus Center and its
business partners.
• Research prospective businesses, industries, and organizations that may benefit from partnering with
or locating at Nmexus Center.
• Help identify new business opportunities and strategic partnerships.
• Assist with outreach to local businesses, contractors, entrepreneurs, educational institutions, and community organizations.
• Help coordinate meetings, presentations, and networking events.
• Prepare business proposals, presentations, reports, and other business development materials.
• Maintain records of business contacts, partnerships, and projects using Monday.com or other CRM software.
• Conduct market research to identify industry trends and business opportunities.
• Support businesses located within Nmexus Center by assisting with projects, research, and resource
coordination.
• Collaborate with leadership on special projects that promote economic development and business
growth.
• Collaborate with other Nmexus Center departments on selected cross-functional projects as
opportunities arise.
• Perform other business development-related duties as assigned.
Preferred Qualifications
• Currently enrolled at Central New Mexico Community College (CNM) in a business-related program.
• Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
• Comfortable speaking with business owners and professionals.
• Strong organizational and time management skills.
• Proficient in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
• Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
• Professional attitude with strong attention to detail.
• Willingness to learn and take initiative.

We're looking for someone who enjoys connecting with people, learning about businesses, and helping
