## Job Summary

As an Enterprise Business Team Intern, you will assist in facilitating teamwork across analytics, engagement, supplier management, and project initiatives teams to ensure alignment and smooth communication.

## Job Description

CAI is looking for an **Enterprise Business Team Intern** who is eager to gain hands-on experience across multiple business functions while helping drive collaboration, process improvement, and operational excellence.

This is a **full-time, remote internship** operating on **EST hours**. The internship runs from **June 7, 2027 through August 6, 2027**.

## Responsibilities

- Assist in facilitating teamwork across analytics, engagement, supplier management, and project initiatives teams to ensure alignment and smooth communication.
- Develop clear and comprehensive documentation of workflows, processes, and collaborative efforts to support operational efficiency.
- Identify bottlenecks, misalignments, or areas of inefficiency within and between teams, and propose actionable solutions.
- Leverage technology and fresh perspectives to recommend strategies for improving processes and fostering collaboration.
- Gain exposure to diverse teams and operations to discover specific strengths and interests, contributing to both personal growth and organizational success.
- Participate in meetings to observe, understand, and learn team functions, fostering collaboration across various sectors of the business to ensure alignment and shared goals.
